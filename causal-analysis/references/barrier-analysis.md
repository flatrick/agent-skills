# Barrier analysis

Use barrier analysis when controls should have prevented, detected, contained, mitigated, or recovered from the outcome.

For each hazard or harmful flow, identify the target, the expected barrier, and its design basis.
Classify the barrier as:

- absent;
- present but failed;
- bypassed or disabled;
- ineffective for the actual conditions;
- successful but insufficient against another path;
- status unresolved.

Record evidence for the barrier's existence, availability, activation, performance, maintenance, and independence.
Distinguish a barrier failure from the upstream reason it was unavailable and from governance that should have verified it.

Map corrective actions to the failed or missing control and define how a future test will demonstrate prevention, detection, containment, or recovery under the relevant conditions.
