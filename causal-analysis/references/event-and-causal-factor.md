# Event-and-causal-factor charting

Use event-and-causal-factor charting when temporal interactions among events and conditions matter.

Build the factual timeline first.
Represent events as bounded occurrences and conditions as states that persist over an interval.
Mark evidence gaps, estimated ordering, and conflicting sources.
Then connect only relationships supported by evidence and label each relationship with its claim ID.

The chart should distinguish what occurred from hypotheses about why.
Earlier events are not automatically causes.
Look for conditions that enabled an event, controls that should have interrupted the sequence, and branches where the available evidence permits more than one ordering or explanation.

Use the chart to test whether the narrative accounts for the outcome, not to make the narrative true through layout.
